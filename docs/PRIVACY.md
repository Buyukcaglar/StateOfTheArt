# Privacy Policy — State of the Art

*Last updated: 25 November 2026*

State of the Art (iPhone, iPad and Mac) does not collect, store, transmit or share any
personal data.

* No accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
* The app makes no network connections. Links on its info screen open in your browser
  only when you tap them.
* The only thing the app stores is your display preference (fit or fill), kept on your
  device and removed when you delete the app.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Buyukcaglar/StateOfTheArt/issues.
